## The build

One time, fixed scope, the same in all three options.

| Software | What it's for |
|---|---|
| GoHighLevel | CRM, pipelines, calendars, forms, automation |
| Zoom | Calls, with links generated off the calendar |
| Fathom | Recording and AI notes on every call |
| Typeform | Qualification answered before anyone picks up |
| Slack | Alerts to the right person at the right moment |
| Notion | SOPs, onboarding, tracking |
| Whop | Checkout, payment plans, failed payment recovery |
| Zapier | The connective tissue between all of it |
| Google Workspace | Email, calendar and file access for your team |
| Loom | Training and SOPs your closers can actually follow |
| Phone and A2P | Registered number so the text reminders send |

If you already use one of these I'll work with what you have. Either way you never have to open the back end.

1. **Lead capture and attribution.** Every lead in one place, tagged to the exact post or ad that brought them in
2. **Speed to lead.** Your setter gets pinged the moment a lead lands. If nobody calls, it escalates
3. **Booking.** Setter and closer calendars stay separate so you can see which path converts
4. **Show up.** Confirmation, a reminder the day before, a short one an hour out. All of it shuts off on its own if the call moves
5. **Call recording and disposition.** Recording on every call, plus a disposition system so you know what happened on each one
6. **No show recovery.** Two automated touches, then it goes to a person
7. **Follow up.** Sequenced by why you lost the deal. Price and timing shouldn't get the same message
8. **Close and collect.** Agreement and payment tracked together. Nothing closes until both land
9. **Failed payment recovery.** You'll know the day a card declines, and the client gets contacted the same day
10. **Reactivation.** Old leads and no shows cycle back on a schedule instead of sitting there dead
11. **Team onboarding and offboarding.** Accounts, access, calendar, training path, first week checklist
12. **Reporting.** One dashboard. Leads by source, speed to lead, show rate, close rate, cash collected against contracted, and who's behind on payment

## Ongoing management

What the monthly covers. It's in Options 1 and 2, and it's the part that doesn't exist in Option 3.

1. Every week you get a report. What happened, what the numbers did, what came in, what I changed
2. One call a week, where we decide what's next
3. I'll watch the numbers and fix what's leaking before you notice it
4. I'll help you hire a setter, a closer, a sales manager, or a CSM. I was a recruiter before this
5. I'll work directly with your marketing person and whoever ends up on fulfillment
6. I'll keep the system current as your offer changes
7. Everything stays documented. You own the system and the documentation

Small adjustments are part of the monthly. When you want something that doesn't exist yet, it goes next in line and I'll scope it before anything starts.

## What's not in scope

Content and ads sit with your marketing person. Your offer and messaging stay yours. Coaching and sales calls stay with you and your team.

For anything you need that I don't do, I'll introduce you to several people and help you pick. Landing pages, ads, webinar strategy, a coach to take fulfillment off you. You get options and my read on each one, not a single name and good luck.
