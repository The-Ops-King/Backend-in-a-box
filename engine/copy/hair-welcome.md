A word on expectations

Hair responds slowly. Meaningful changes from lifestyle work and any medical treatments can take time. Our goal is by weeks 2 to 4, you'll see less hair coming out in the shower. This is the first sign the protocol is active. By month 6, most clients see 30 to 50% less scalp visibility in their progress photos. By weeks 12 to 24, existing follicles that were thinning start producing visibly thicker hair. So your consistency matters far more than intensity. We'll set clear, realistic milestones together on our first call so you always know what "on track" looks like.
