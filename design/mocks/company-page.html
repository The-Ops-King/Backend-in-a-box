Company page, five structures

A company's page: every workflow it has, in journey order. Same Slate colors, same sixteen workflows (Hair's), five structures. Each row is the switch, the name, where it came from, and three counts (people, in flight, failed); on a phone the counts fold into a short note under the name. Tap a row to open the workflow; tap a switch to flip it.
